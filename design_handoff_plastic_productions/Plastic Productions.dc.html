Plastic Productions is a Berlin collective running art and performance nights for artists who don't have a gallery, an agent or a following yet. Placeholder copy — replace with the real origin story.

Every night is built around what the artist actually needs: a wall, a floor, a PA, ninety minutes of everyone's attention. We handle the space, the tech, the door and the crowd. Nobody pays to play and nobody is booked for their follower count.

Now we take that roster to brands. Not because the events need funding to exist, but because the artists deserve paid work that doesn't ask them to flatten what they do.
